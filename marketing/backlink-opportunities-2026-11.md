# HumanoidOnline — Free Backlink Opportunities (researched 2026-11-01)

## Step 1 — What the site is

- **What it is:** a market-intelligence catalogue for humanoid robots, built for buyers. It is not a news site. It tracks 71 platforms (51 published) and 29 makers (23 published). 19 robots are commercially accessible now, and live rental offers are listed.
- **What makes it different:** maturity, obtainability and evidence are tracked as three separate facts. Every commercial fact has an evidence source and a confidence level. "Unknown stays unknown" (no made-up prices).
- **Pages worth linking to:** `/robots/*` profiles, `/compare`, `/manufacturers`, `/use-cases/*` (8 applications: events, healthcare, home, manufacturing, research & education, retail, security, warehouse), `/research`, and the "Find a Humanoid" matching wizard.
- **Audience:** operators, procurement and innovation teams, integrators, researchers and educators, and investors and analysts.
- **Natural reasons people would link:** published price and rental data (e.g. AgiBot A2 Ultra rental at €6,500/week via a distributor, NEURA 4NE1 Mini at €19,999). Also the deployment-evidence view, which separates pilots from announcements, and a use-case lens that suits industry and education audiences.
- **Fit for a US audience:** the parent company is in Prague and prices are in EUR. For US outreach, lead with US-relevant pages: Agility Digit (RaaS), Apptronik, Figure, Tesla Optimus, 1X, Boston Dynamics, and the warehouse and manufacturing use cases.

## How I verified things (read this first)

- **Live:** each URL returned HTTP 200 on 2026-11-01 (a few were checked in a real browser because they sit behind Cloudflare).
- **Link attribute:** I read the `rel` attribute on the site's *existing* outbound links of the same kind: in-article links (via the site's RSS feed or a rendered article), listing-page links, or profile website links.
  - **Followed (observed)** means comparable links carried no `nofollow`/`ugc`/`sponsored` on the day I checked. That is evidence, not a guarantee: editors can change it per link.
  - **Nofollow (observed)** means comparable links carried `nofollow`/`ugc`.
  - **Stated** means the site's own policy page says so.
  - **Unknown** means the page blocked my check or I found no comparable link. I did not guess.
- I did not use domain-authority numbers anywhere.
- **⚑ flag** marks something I could not fully verify. It's noted on the row.
- Result: **40 verified opportunities, not 50.** The rules say to return fewer rather than pad. Directories and competitor-link targets were the thin categories. Most free "directories" are generic SEO lists, or charge for the followed link.

## Step 5 — Ranking method

Each site was ranked on five factors, in this order of weight: niche relevance, then likelihood of approval, ease, referral traffic, and trust.

- **Top ranks** are humanoid-specific editorial sites where followed links were observed and an open contribution channel exists: Humanoid Robotics Technology, RoboticsTomorrow, Robohub and Humanoid Sports Network.
- **GitHub lists and Hugging Face rank high despite nofollow**, because researchers actually click through from them.
- **Generic launch directories rank low** despite being easy. They send little relevant traffic.
- **The .edu guides sit in the middle.** Each is valuable, but approval is unlikely.
