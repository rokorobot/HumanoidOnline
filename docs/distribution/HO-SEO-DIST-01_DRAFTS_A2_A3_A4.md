| Robots with **no offer on file anywhere** | 19 | 19 | 19 |
| Evidenced deployments in region | 0 | 2 (Digit @ GXO; Figure 02 @ BMW, completed) | 1 (Walker S2 pilot @ Hitachi China) |
| Offer confidence | all MEDIUM, no human verification recorded | several "no human verification recorded" | all MEDIUM, no human verification recorded |

Method points that are safe to quote: global offers don't count as regional
availability; deployments never make a robot purchasable; unknown prices stay
"not published"; confidence is never upgraded by publication.

**Wording rule:** say "evidence-linked offer" or "offer on file", never "verified
availability". Offers are MEDIUM confidence without human verification.
