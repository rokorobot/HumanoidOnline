Title: Maturity, obtainability, evidence: a framework for evaluating humanoid robots

Summary: Humanoid robot coverage blurs three separate questions: how capable a robot is, whether you can actually obtain it, and whether it is deployed anywhere. This article sets out a simple framework for keeping them apart and applies it to 52 published humanoid profiles. Only 5 have a confirmed offer in North America (Nov 2026), and the gap between demos and deployments is wide. Intermediate level, aimed at AI and robotics leaders evaluating platforms.
