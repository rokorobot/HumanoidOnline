Title: Free evidence-linked catalogue of humanoid robots (availability, prices, deployments)

Body:
Hi all. For anyone evaluating humanoid platforms for research or deployment: we built HumanoidOnline (https://humanoidonline.com), a free catalogue of 51 published humanoid robot profiles. It keeps technical maturity, commercial availability (purchase, rental, RaaS) and deployment evidence as separate facts, each linked to a source with an observation date. Unknown values stay unknown.

The research and education view may be the most useful here: https://humanoidonline.com/use-cases/research-education

Corrections and missing platforms are very welcome. (Disclosure: I run the project.)
