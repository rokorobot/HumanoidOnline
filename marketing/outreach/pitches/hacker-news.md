Hi HN. Humanoid robot announcements are everywhere, but it's surprisingly hard to tell which robots you can actually buy, rent or get through robots-as-a-service. We built a catalogue that keeps three things separate: technical maturity, commercial obtainability and deployment evidence. Every price or availability fact links to its source with an observation date, and anything unverified is shown as unknown rather than estimated.

As of 10 November 2026: 52 published humanoid profiles, and only 5 have a confirmed offer in North America.

Happy to answer questions about the data and the method.
