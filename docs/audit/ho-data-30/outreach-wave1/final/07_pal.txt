I run HumanoidOnline (humanoidonline.com), an independent catalogue of humanoid robots. We publish only manufacturer-supported specifications and show anything we cannot source as "unknown" - we never estimate.

We have read pal-robotics.com and the TALOS datasheet. We could not find a REEM-C datasheet (the /datasheet/reem-c link opens the TIAGo datasheet). The following values are not stated in the documents we found:

REEM-C
1. Battery energy (Wh)
2. Standard end effector (model) and degrees of freedom per hand
3. Cameras / depth sensors fitted (yes/no; which)
4. Programming API available to customers (beyond ROS) (yes/no)
5. Spoken conversation with users (yes/no) - we found text-to-speech in many languages, but not two-way conversation
6. Arm span, fingertip to fingertip (cm) and single-arm reach, shoulder axis to gripper tip (cm)

TALOS
7. Rated walking speed on flat ground (m/s)
8. Standard gripper model as shipped
9. Programming API available to customers (beyond ROS / ros_control) (yes/no)
10. Spoken conversation with users (yes/no)
11. Arm span (cm) and single-arm reach (cm)

A REEM-C datasheet would answer most of the REEM-C items at once. Please reply from a PAL address or point to a public page or PDF on your domain. We will show the values as manufacturer statements with the date of your reply and can share the entries with you before they go live.
