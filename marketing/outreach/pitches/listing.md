Long description:
HumanoidOnline is commercial intelligence for the humanoid robot market. It tracks 72 humanoid robots (52 published profiles) from 23 published manufacturers and keeps three facts separate: technical maturity, commercial obtainability (purchase, rental, lease, robots-as-a-service) and real-world deployment evidence. Every price and availability claim links to its source with an observation date and confidence level; missing data is shown as unknown, never estimated. Buyers can compare robots side by side, explore 8 use cases (from warehouse logistics to research and education), and read dated regional availability research for North America, Europe and Asia.
