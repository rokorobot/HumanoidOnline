Working title: Specifying humanoid robots for industrial automation: what the data shows
Abstract: Humanoid robots are entering automation conversations, but published specifications and commercial availability are uneven. Drawing on an evidence-linked catalogue of 52 published humanoid profiles, the article summarises what is typically published (payload, height, mobility) and what is usually missing. It also covers how few platforms have confirmed offers in North America (5 as of Nov 2026), and the questions automation engineers should ask vendors.
