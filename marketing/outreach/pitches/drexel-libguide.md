Your Robotics LibGuide is a helpful starting point for students. I'd like to suggest a free resource that may be useful for projects involving humanoid robots:

HumanoidOnline: https://humanoidonline.com/robots
A free, source-cited reference for 52 published humanoid robot profiles: specifications, commercial status and deployment evidence. Every fact links to its original source, and missing values are shown as unknown rather than estimated, which also makes it useful for teaching source evaluation. There is also a research and education page listing robots used in that setting:
https://humanoidonline.com/use-cases/research-education

No login or payment is required. Thank you for considering it.
